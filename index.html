Tidewise

A personal budgeting app that links your bank accounts to track and categorize spending.

What this is

Tidewise is a single-user, personal finance application. It uses Plaid to securely connect bank accounts, sync transactions, and help track spending by category against a monthly budget.

Data & privacy

Tidewise is built and used by a single individual for personal budgeting. All transaction and account data is stored locally and is not shared with any third party beyond Plaid, which is used solely to connect to financial institutions on the user's behalf.

Tidewise can also read the user's own Gmail with read-only access, solely to find purchase receipts from stores and match them to card charges. Receipt contents are stored locally on the user's computer, are never sent to or shared with anyone else, and are not used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
